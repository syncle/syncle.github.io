Jaesik Park is an Associate Professor of Computer Science Engineering and an Interdisciplinary Program in AI at Seoul National University. He earned his Bachelor’s degree from Hanyang University, and his Master’s and Ph.D. degrees from KAIST, supervised by Prof. Yu-Wing Tai and Prof. In So Kweon. Prior to his current role, he was a faculty member at POSTECH and a staff research scientist at Intel intelligent systems lab (led by Dr. Vladlen Koltun), where he co-created Open3D library. His research interests encompass image, video, and 3D synthesis, as well as 3D perception and robot vision problems. He serves as an Associate Editor for IEEE TPAMI and an Action Editor for TMLR, and regularly contributes to the program committees of prestigious international conferences such as CVPR, ECCV, ICCV, ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, SIGGRAPH, SIGGRAPH Asia, and ICRA.
